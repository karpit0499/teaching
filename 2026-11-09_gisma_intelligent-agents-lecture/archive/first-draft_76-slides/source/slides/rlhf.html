How Assistants Learn What We Prefer

[Murphy 2023, §22.4.1, pp. 815–816]

47

RLHF: reinforcement learning from human feedback, in three steps.

1 · Humans rank

Raters compare two itineraries and pick the better one.

2 · Learn a scorer

A reward model learns to predict which answer people prefer.

3 · Tune the model

RL fine-tuning makes the LLM score highly with that scorer.

Can be hacked too: models learn to be verbose, agreeable or overconfident because raters liked it.

This is how the model was trained, not what happens when the agent runs.

Most assistant models are aligned this way. People compare more reliably than they score, so the reward model learns from comparisons: preference learning. Then the language model is fine-tuned with RL, for example PPO, to score well while staying close to its original behaviour. PML2 §22.4.1, pp. 815–816; §35.3.4, pp. 1148–1149.
