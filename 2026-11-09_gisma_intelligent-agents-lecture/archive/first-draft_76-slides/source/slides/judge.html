A judge checks against explicit criteria

Returns structured feedback: what failed, evidence, who fixes it

The loop must be bounded

e.g. one draft plus two revisions

Plus time and cost limits

A judge is a second opinion, not an oracle

Same model → may share the same mistakes

Test it against careful human judgements

Structured judge feedback

verdict: "revise"

failed: "budget"

evidence: "680 € > 600 €"

overrun: 80 €

responsible: ["Budget", "Planner"]

iteration: 1 of 3

No infinite loops: cap it, then hand to a human.

The judge returns structured feedback so the supervisor can route fixes to the responsible agents. The loop is capped. A judge LLM can be wrong; if it shares the model with the agents it evaluates, it may share their blind spots, so its reliability must itself be tested.
