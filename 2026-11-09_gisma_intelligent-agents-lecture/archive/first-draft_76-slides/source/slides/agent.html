The Agent Cycle

[Part 1 · What agents are]

7

An agent perceives, decides under uncertainty, acts, and learns from what happens.

Perceive

Screenshot shows a hotel at 95 €

Decide

Price may be stale: check it first

Act

Call the price tool

Observe

Tool says 110 €. Update the belief.

Repeat until the goal is met or a stop rule fires

Every agent, from a thermostat to an LLM travel planner, runs this cycle. Every failure can be located in it: misreading the screenshot is a perception failure; booking without checking is a decision failure; saying “booked” when nothing was booked is a feedback failure.
