Online learning (§19.7.5)

Update the model as data arrives, bit by bit

Continual learning

Gain new skills over time without losing old ones

Catastrophic forgetting (§19.7.4)

Training on new data overwrites what was learned

Drift

Domain drift: the data changes

Concept drift: what the task means changes

Online learning updates incrementally as data arrives. Continual learning means acquiring new knowledge without forgetting; the central difficulty is catastrophic forgetting. §19.7 also covers domain and concept drift.
