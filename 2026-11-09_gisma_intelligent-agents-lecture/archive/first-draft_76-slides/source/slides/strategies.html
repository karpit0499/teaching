UCB: optimism in the face of uncertainty

Imagine each option’s plausible best value

Pick the highest: rarely tried options get a chance

Thompson sampling: the Bayesian way

Draw one imagined value per option from beliefs

Pick the best draw, observe, update

Each option is chosen as often as it’s likely to be best

Both keep regret growing slowly

UCB adds an uncertainty bonus: options tried rarely have wide ranges and a high plausible best, so they get explored; options tried often and doing poorly drop out.
