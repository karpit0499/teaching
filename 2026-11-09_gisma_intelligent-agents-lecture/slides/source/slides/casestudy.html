Case study · as reported by CNN, citing anonymous sources

A chatbot analyses a ship’s manifest

AI turns it into an intelligence report

Missing: verify and escalate

Intercept of a Chinese vessel planned

The report falsely claimed the ship carried nuclear-weapons components. One source said it “almost started a war.” No on-record official confirmation was reported.

The failure was the system design, not just the model.

Per CNN’s report, the incident happened in spring 2026 at Special Operations Command Pacific. AI outputs were chained together without verification, and nothing triggered escalation before a consequential action was planned. In this lecture’s terms: no deterministic or human check, no value-of-information thinking about verifying before acting, and no stop rule proportionate to the stakes.
