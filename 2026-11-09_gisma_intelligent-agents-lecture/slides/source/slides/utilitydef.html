Utility: how good an outcome is

Measured for the person the agent serves

Lets very different outcomes be compared

Rational agents maximise expected utility

Weigh each outcome by its probability

Pick the best average (Bayesian decision theory, §34.1.3)

Neither optimist nor pessimist

Don’t chase the best case

Don’t just play it safe either

The recipe

1

List the possible actions

2

List the outcomes and how likely each is

3

Score each outcome: its utility

4

Average the scores, weighted by probability

5

Pick the action with the best average

A utility is a number expressing how good an outcome is for the person served. A rational agent chooses the action whose outcomes, weighted by probability, are best on average: maximising expected utility. Next slide: the worked example.
