The objective decides who is served well

Maximising average satisfaction can fail a minority

e.g. travellers who need step-free routes

Fairness as constraints on decisions

Equal opportunity: among people who truly qualify, “yes” at equal rates across groups

Equalised odds: correct and wrong “yes” rates equal across groups

Definitions can conflict

Often they can’t all hold: choosing is a value judgement

If the agent maximises average satisfaction, it may consistently serve a minority badly. PML2 describes fairness definitions as decision-theoretic constraints; several reasonable definitions exist and often cannot all be satisfied at once.
