What Is an Intelligent Agent?

[Part 1 · Definition]

6

An agent pursues a goal in a loop

Perceives its environment through observations

Keeps a picture of the situation, with uncertainty

Acts, then observes the consequences

The loop defines it, not the technology

Thermostats, chess programs and LLM planners are all agents

They differ in how good their decisions are

“Agent” describes how a system is organised

Not a guarantee that it is reliable or intelligent

A bad agent can loop forever or invent facts confidently

Thermostat

Perceives: Senses temperature

Acts: Switches heating

Chess program

Perceives: Sees the board

Acts: Moves a piece

Self-driving car

Perceives: Cameras, radar

Acts: Steers, brakes

Travel planner

Perceives: Messages, search results

Acts: Searches, plans, asks

Same loop. Very different decision quality.

An intelligent agent perceives, keeps an internal picture, chooses actions toward a goal, acts and observes the consequences. The technology inside does not define it: a thermostat and an LLM travel planner both qualify. What separates them is how sophisticated their decisions are and how much uncertainty they handle. Calling something an agent says nothing about whether it is reliable.
