People compare better than they score

“Which of these two is better?” is easy

“Rate this from 1 to 10” is noisy

Preference learning

The reward model learns a score from comparisons

RL fine-tuning, on a leash

e.g. PPO (§35.3.4) pushes toward high scores…

…while staying close to the original model

Trained once, not while running

At run time the weights are fixed

Itinerary A

3 museums/day, rushed, 598 €

Itinerary B

2 museums/day, long lunches, 560 €

Rater prefers B

LLM agents are not automatically RL agents.

Raters compare responses more reliably than they score them, so the reward model learns from comparisons. The LLM is then fine-tuned with RL, for example PPO, to score well while staying close to its original behaviour. This happens during training; when the agent runs, it is not learning from rewards.
