Value of Information

[Murphy 2023, §34.2, pp. 1099–1102]

22

Gathering information is an action

Calling a tool, searching, asking the user

It costs time, money or patience

Worth it only if it could change the decision

Two indoor museums? The forecast changes nothing: worth 0

Imagine a perfect forecast

Sun (60%): pick the hike, get 10

Rain (40%): pick the museum, get 6

Average: 0.6 × 10 + 0.4 × 6 = 8.4

Check the forecast

Sun 60%

Hike: 10

Rain 40%

Museum: 6

8.4 with the forecast vs 6.8 without

Information is valuable only to the extent that it could change your decision. With a perfect forecast the agent picks the hike on sunny days and the museum on rainy days, averaging 8.4. Next slide: the value of that information.
