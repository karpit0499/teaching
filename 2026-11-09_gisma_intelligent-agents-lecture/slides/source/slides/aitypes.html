Same Model, Three Kinds of System

[Part 1 · What agents are]

9

Traditional AI

Predicts or classifies

“Will this hotel get more expensive next month?”

Generative AI

Creates content

“Write me a guide to Lisbon’s museums.”

Agentic AI

Acts, observes, adapts

Searches, checks the budget, revises the plan, knows when to stop.

“Agentic” describes the system around the model, not the model.

The same LLM can sit inside all three. Classify a review: predictive. Write an itinerary in one go: generative. Put it in a loop where it searches, checks and revises: agentic.
