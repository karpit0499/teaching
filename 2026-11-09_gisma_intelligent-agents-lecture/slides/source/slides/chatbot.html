From Chatbot to Agent

[Part 1 · Definition]

8

A chatbot

You: “Where should I go for 3 days?”

Bot: “You should visit Lisbon!”

✗ Produces text

✗ Takes no actions

✗ Never sees a consequence

An agent

1

Searches hotels

2

Adds up the cost: 680 €

3

Notices: over budget

4

Swaps to a cheaper hotel

5

New total: 590 € ✓

An agent acts, observes the result, and changes what it does next.

The same model can be either. Give the chatbot the ability to search hotels, check the total against the budget, notice the overrun and revise, and it becomes an agent: it acts, observes the result of its action and changes what it does next.
