Planning: look ahead before acting

Simulate action sequences and their consequences

Value iteration: start with rough guesses of each state’s value…

…improve them until they stop changing

Feedback: correct after acting

See what actually happened and adjust

Good agents do both

Plan with current beliefs, then act, observe, update, replan

Planning looks ahead with the agent’s current model; value iteration repeatedly asks “what is the best I can do from here, given my current guesses for the next states?” until the guesses stop changing. Feedback corrects after acting. Good agents combine both.
