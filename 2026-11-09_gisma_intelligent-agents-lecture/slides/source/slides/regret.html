Regret: what you lost by not knowing the best option

Compare with an agent that knew it from the start

Explore too little

Stuck on a mediocre option: regret grows steadily

Explore too much

Keep paying for needless experiments: regret grows too

Good strategies

Regret grows more and more slowly as they focus

Best possible, if you had known: 100

Regret = 100 − 82 = 18 (illustrative numbers)

Low regret = learning fast what’s best

Regret is the gap between what the agent achieved and what it would have achieved knowing the best option from the start. It is the natural yardstick for exploration strategies. PML2 also uses it in §34.3.1.
