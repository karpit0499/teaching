Weigh Outcomes by Likelihood

[Murphy 2023, §34.1,
pp. 1093–1098]

23

Your class party: outdoors or indoors? The forecast says 60% sun, 40% rain.

Outdoors

Sunny (60%) → 10 · Rainy (40%) → 2

0.6 × 10 + 0.4 × 2

6.8

Best average

Indoors

Sunny → 6 · Rainy → 6

0.6 × 6 + 0.4 × 6

6.0

Not the best case. Not the safest. The best average: expected utility.

A rational agent weighs each outcome by its probability. Outdoors averages 6.8, indoors 6.0, so go outdoors. It neither chases the best possible outcome nor plays it safe.
