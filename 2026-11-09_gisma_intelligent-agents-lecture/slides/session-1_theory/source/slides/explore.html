Explore or Exploit?

[Murphy 2023, §34.4.3, §34.4.7]

27

Every pull of the lever gives a reward and some information.

Exploit

Use the option that looks best so far

Explore

Try an uncertain option that might be better

Regret

What you lose by not knowing the best option from the start

Your favourite restaurant vs the new place next door

Pure exploitation can lock you onto a mediocre option forever; pure exploration wastes effort. Both make regret grow steadily. Good strategies explore more when knowledge is thin and less as it grows. For agents: which source, tool or provider to rely on across many tasks. Photo: Cindy F, Unsplash.
