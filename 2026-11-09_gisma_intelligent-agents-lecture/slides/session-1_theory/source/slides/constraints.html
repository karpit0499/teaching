Rules Are Not Wishes

[Part 2 · Hard constraints vs preferences]

24

Must: hard constraints

  • Total cost at most 600 €
  • Wheelchair accessible
  • Ends by 22:00: the venue’s rule

Valid or invalid. Never traded off.

Would like: preferences

  • Live music
  • Good food
  • Close to campus

Better or worse. Traded off against cost.

A 680 € party isn’t “better”. It’s invalid.

A rational agent maximises expected utility within the plans that satisfy every hard constraint. Preferences shape the score; constraints shape the space. This is why, in Session 2, the budget is checked by code, not by the LLM.
