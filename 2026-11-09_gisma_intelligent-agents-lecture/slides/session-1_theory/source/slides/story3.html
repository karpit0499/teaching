What Happened in October 2026

[Tech Times, 27 Oct 2026 · OpenAI preprint]

10

The Exponential Time Hypothesis: a classic logic puzzle (3-SAT) can’t be solved in sub-exponential time

An internal OpenAI model claims a disproof; hours later, Anthropic publishes one from a Claude model

Both proofs come with Lean code: a computer checks every step

Human experts, including ETH’s originators, are reviewing: “not certification”

About 800 papers build on ETH. The algorithm only wins at astronomical sizes.

Even a breakthrough needs a checker: a proof assistant, then human experts.

Claims, not settled results: as of late October 2026 the proofs were not peer-reviewed, and the review panel (Impagliazzo, Paturi, Williams) said its involvement should not be taken as certification. Both proofs are Lean-verified; the result does not resolve P vs NP and has no practical speed-up at realistic sizes. The point for us: the same kind of system that nearly caused an incident is producing frontier research, and both need checking.
